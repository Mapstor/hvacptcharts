    body: |-
      R-454B is a zeotropic blend with a small glide, so suction is read on the

      {low} psig and moves far less than the high side. Below {low} psig points to
      weak airflow, a restriction, or an undercharge; above it points to an
      overcharge or a compressor problem. Use the
