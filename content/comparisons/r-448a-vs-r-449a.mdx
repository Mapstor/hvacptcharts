## R-448A vs R-449A: peer alternatives, not source and target

The R-404A retrofit market split cleanly around 2015-2017 between two competing A1 HFC/HFO blends: Honeywell's Solstice N40 (R-448A) and Chemours's Opteon XP40 (R-449A). Both target the same commercial refrigeration application space, both deliver approximately 65% GWP reduction vs R-404A, both preserve A1 safety and POE lubricant compatibility, and both retrofit R-404A equipment without component replacement in the typical case.

Because the two blends are close on every technical axis that matters for the retrofit decision, the choice between them reduces to supply-chain standardization, OEM approval status for the specific equipment being serviced, and local reclaim / distributor availability. There is no meaningful service-time retrofit path FROM one TO the other — they are peer targets from R-404A, not source-target of each other.
